**Description (paste as is):**

Weil's criterion states that the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent to the positivity of a quadratic form, built from the primes and the archimedean place through the explicit formula, on all test functions. Restricting the test functions to those supported in [1/λ, λ] gives a form W_λ that depends only on the prime powers up to λ². This preprint studies W_λ for λ between 2 and 6 by high-precision computation, entirely on the prime side (no zero of ζ enters the construction), and proves four statements.

(i) Detection: a zero at 1/2 + δ + iγ contributes 4 Re Φ(γ − iδ)² to W_λ(f); W_λ becomes indefinite for any δ > 0 when γ lies below the resolution height H(λ) = 2πλ², where the zero density first reaches the Nyquist density of the test functions, and detection is exponentially suppressed above it. (ii) Reconstruction: the minimiser of W_λ has a Fourier transform whose real zeros coincide with the zeros of ζ below H(λ); with the primes up to 25 the first 38 zeros are recovered, the first to 105 digits, and the accuracy decays linearly in height to nothing near 2H(λ). (iii) Structure: the minimiser equals Riemann's function Φ multiplied by a smooth taper on [−log λ, log λ]. (iv) Theorems, in ball arithmetic (Arb): W_2(f) ≥ 1.38 × 10⁻¹³ ‖f‖² for every even f supported in [−log 2, log 2], which encloses the exact positivity margin between 1.38 × 10⁻¹³ and 7.6 × 10⁻¹³ (an independent certification, at L = log 2, of a theorem of X. Zhu, arXiv:2608.24827, valid for L ≤ 0.8); and a certified rigidity statement: the same form becomes indefinite when Λ(2) is changed by a relative 2.6 × 10⁻¹¹ or log 2 is shifted by 2.3 × 10⁻¹². Under RH, W_λ is coercive at every scale by Beurling's sampling theorem, and a small margin localises the minimiser's zeros. Nothing here bears on the Riemann Hypothesis beyond the finite scales treated.

The companion repository (scripts, results, and the certificates of Theorems 1–3) is at https://github.com/davidangularme/scale-resolved-weil-positivity. Computations and drafting were assisted by Claude (Anthropic).
